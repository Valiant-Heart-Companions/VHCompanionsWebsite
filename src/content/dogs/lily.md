Lily joined our program at 9 weeks from a Golden Retriever line with a history of therapy and service work. Now in month 10 of training, she has shown exceptional aptitude for nightmare interruption — she responds to the earliest auditory cues of distress during sleep with a reliability that her trainers find uncommon at this stage of training.

Her natural warmth and calm demeanor make her particularly well-suited to recipients whose trauma symptoms include social withdrawal and difficulties with trust. She is currently completing advanced public access training.
