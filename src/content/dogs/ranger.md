Ranger came to us from a reputable working-dog breeder in Virginia at 8 weeks old and passed his puppy temperament assessment with flying colors. He is currently in month 14 of his training program, and his trainers are consistently impressed by his natural attentiveness to his handlers' emotional states.

He is particularly skilled at deep pressure therapy, where his calm body weight and unhurried temperament make him an ideal candidate for recipients whose primary presenting symptoms involve acute anxiety or panic episodes. He is expected to complete training within the next four months.
