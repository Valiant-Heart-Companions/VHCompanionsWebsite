Make A Donation

When you donate to Valiant Heart Companions, you're directly supporting:

  • Specialized training of service dogs for trauma recovery
  • Matching dogs with survivors in need
  • Ongoing support for service dog teams
  • Public education about trauma recovery and service dogs

Your generous contribution, no matter the size, makes a significant impact in helping survivors reclaim their lives.

Valiant Heart Companions is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 82-4410052). All donations are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law.

Other Ways to Support

Monthly Giving

Become a Valiant Heart Guardian with a monthly donation. Recurring gifts provide reliable support that helps us plan effectively for the future and sustain our training program year-round.

Corporate Matching

Many employers match charitable donations. Ask your HR department if your company offers matching gifts — you can double or even triple your impact at no extra cost to you.

Legacy Giving

Include Valiant Heart Companions in your estate planning to create a lasting legacy of healing and support for survivors for generations to come.

In-Kind Donations

We welcome donations of supplies, equipment, and professional services. Please contact us to discuss your potential in-kind contribution.
